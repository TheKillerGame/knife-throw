# Knife Throw

A one-tap phone game. A target spins at the top of the screen; tap to throw a
knife into it. Hit a knife that's already stuck and the run is over. Use up all
your knives and the target breaks: on to the next stage.

By **RisingForce**.

## Play

Open `index.html` in any browser, on a phone or a computer. It's a single file
with no install, no server and no internet needed (the font is embedded and the
sounds are made in code).

Tap anywhere to throw. On a keyboard, use Space, Enter or the Up arrow.

## Features

- Stages get harder: more knives, knives already stuck in the target, and spin
  that speeds up, swings back and forth, or stops and starts
- Every 5th stage is a boss with a darker target and nastier spin, worth +5
- 8 knives to unlock by reaching stages 2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 15 and 20
- Hit, crash and break sounds, plus vibration on phones
- Best score, best stage, chosen knife and mute are remembered
